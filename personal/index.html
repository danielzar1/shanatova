One · the apple

Dip the apple in the honey

Drag the apple down into the jar and lift it out again. Go on, get it properly sticky.

Sweet. May your year be this sticky with good things.

Two · the shofar

Sound the shofar

A hundred blasts are sounded on Rosh Hashanah. Here are the four. Tap one, or press and hold the last for a great blast.

You heard the call. Wake up, it's a new year.

Three · the pomegranate

Open the pomegranate

Tradition says a pomegranate holds 613 seeds, one for every mitzvah. Tap it and see what they spell.

613 seeds. May your merits be as many.

From our family to yours

Wishing you and your family a blessed Yom Tov filled with good health, joy, happiness, love, peace and all Hashem's richest blessings.

Shana Tova U'Metuka. Chag Sameach.
